Mira had a red cup and a blue cup. She filled the red cup with water and carried it to the little tree. The tree was dry, so Mira poured the water slowly. Her brother Leo brought the blue cup and helped her. When they finished, they put both cups by the door and went inside for lunch.

A small dog named Pip found a yellow ball under the table. Pip pushed the ball with his nose. The ball rolled into the hall and stopped beside a shoe. Ana picked up the ball, tossed it across the room, and Pip ran after it. After three throws, Pip was tired and rested on the rug.

Tom wanted to make a paper boat. He folded a square sheet, pressed every edge, and drew a tiny star on the side. Outside, a shallow stream ran past the garden. Tom placed the boat on the water. It floated past two stones, turned near a leaf, and stopped at a small branch. Tom lifted it out and carried it home to dry.

Lina woke before the rain began. She saw dark clouds through the window and remembered that her toy rabbit was still in the yard. Lina ran outside, picked up the rabbit, and came back just as the first drops fell. She dried the toy with a towel and set it on a chair. Then she watched the rain from the warm kitchen.

Ben and Noor built a tower from wooden blocks. Ben placed the wide blocks at the bottom, and Noor added smaller blocks above them. The tower leaned to one side. They removed the top block and tried again. This time the tower stood straight. They counted twelve blocks before carefully taking the tower apart.

A green kite was stuck in a low tree. Sam could not reach it, so he asked his aunt for help. She brought a long pole and gently lifted the string from a branch. The kite fell onto the grass without tearing. Sam checked the paper, tied the string again, and waited for the wind to return.

Ella made soup with her father. He washed the carrots while Ella counted potatoes. They cut the vegetables, put them in a pot, and added water. While the soup cooked, Ella set three bowls on the table. The kitchen smelled warm and sweet. When the soup was ready, they waited a little before tasting it.

A tiny bird landed on the fence beside Omar. The bird looked at the crumbs in his hand but did not come closer. Omar placed the crumbs on the ground and stepped back. The bird hopped down, ate one crumb, then another, and flew to a nearby roof. Omar smiled and swept the remaining crumbs into a small pile.

Nia had a box of crayons with one empty space. She looked under her desk, inside her bag, and behind a book. At last she found the missing purple crayon under a sheet of paper. Nia put it back in the box, drew a large purple moon, and added silver stars around it. She wrote her name at the bottom of the page.

Kai and his sister June walked to the pond after breakfast. They saw three ducks near the reeds and a frog on a flat stone. June spoke softly so the frog would not jump away. Kai drew the pond in a notebook. On the way home, they compared the drawing with the shapes of the clouds above them.

Rosa heard a quiet sound from the porch. A cardboard box was moving near the steps. She opened the top and found a kitten hiding inside. Rosa brought a bowl of water and called her neighbor. The neighbor knew the kitten and carried it home. Later, Rosa folded the empty box and put it with the recycling.

Max had two socks that looked almost the same. One had a thin gray stripe and the other had a thick gray stripe. He searched the laundry basket for their partners. After matching the stripes, Max made two pairs and put them in the drawer. The last sock belonged to his father, so Max left it on the bed.
